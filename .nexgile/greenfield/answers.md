# Requirements brief — Construction And Property Management
## Profile
An internal construction and real estate management application connecting project delivery, procurement, finance, property sales, and controlled staff access. · internal tool · Construction operations and real estate sales management · size: large · depth: thorough · money, personalData, files
- **Has a user interface?** Yes (The specification requires 36 routable Angular pages with real API-backed actions.)
- **Runs locally?** Yes (Go can serve the Angular pages and API locally with SQLite and attachments stored on the machine.)
## The app
Build an internal Angular, Go and SQLite construction and real estate application with all 36 specified pages, seven server-enforced staff roles, project-scoped access, and connected site, procurement, finance, sales and collection workflows. Deliver local execution first with the specified architectural branding and IBM Plex Sans, auditable approvals, persistent records, working recovery, and real-backend acceptance checks across every required journey.

2. The Go API checks persisted credentials, account status, role, and project assignments.
3. Create a rotated secure server session and return minimal identity and effective permissions.

5. Open an authorized internal return route or the role's specified landing page.
6. Enforce permissions on every protected page, API, action, export, and document.

- Rate-limited attempts
- Distinct safe connectivity errors
- Access-denied and no-projects-assigned states
- Expired sessions require sign-in without replaying financial or stock mutations

5. Complete the password change and invalidate old sessions.
6. On logout invalidate the server session and clear authenticated UI state.
- X2 Reject expired or previously consumed setup/recovery links; allow an Administrator to issue a replacement after identity verification through an established trusted channel. [assumed, high impact] (assumption)
### W3 Project to site progress (release 1) — actors: R2 Project Manager, R3 Site Engineer; trigger: A project is ready for planning and site reporting.; outcome: Project views reconcile approved activity and weighted progress. (doc:Greenfield-Angular-Go-SQLite-Requirements.md)

2. Record approved work or expense.
3. Record a linked payable bill.
4. Allocate a manual payment.

### W6 Enquiry to booking (release 1) — actors: R6 Sales Executive; trigger: A prospective buyer enquires.; outcome: One active reservation/booking relationship protects each unit. (doc:Greenfield-Angular-Go-SQLite-Requirements.md)
1. Create a lead.
2. Schedule and record a site visit.
3. Reserve an available unit for a customer.
4. Convert to a booking and customer while preserving the lead history.
5. Make the unit unavailable to another buyer.
- Simultaneous booking attempts produce one success and one clear conflict
- Possible duplicate contacts generate warnings without treating shared phone numbers as proof of identity
- X6 A losing concurrent reservation or booking attempt receives a conflict and refreshed availability; preserve the lead and entered contact details without acquiring the unit. [assumed, high impact] (assumption)
### W7 Booking to collection (release 1) — actors: R5 Finance Officer, R6 Sales Executive, Authorized cancellation approver; trigger: A booking requires installment billing or cancellation.; outcome: Collections and refunds remain traceable and balances reconcile. 1. Create installment invoices.
2. Record incoming receipts.
3. Allocate receipts to permitted invoices.
4. Display the remaining balance.

6. Record any refund separately while preserving original receipts and sale history.
- X7 Reject excess or incompatible allocations without partial posting; if cancellation terms are missing, block refund calculation and require independently approved terms before proceeding. [assumed, high impact] (assumption)
### W8 Inspection to resolution (release 1) — actors: R3 Site Engineer, R2 Project Manager; trigger: An inspection is performed.; outcome: Failed inspections have traceable corrective action. (doc:Greenfield-Angular-Go-SQLite-Requirements.md)
1. Record checklist results.
2. Create an assigned corrective issue for a failed item.
3. Track the issue through work in progress.
4. Resolve it with evidence.
5. Retain inspection and issue history.
6. Allow authorized reopening.
- X8 A failed inspection item cannot be closed without a linked corrective issue; a reviewer can reopen a purported resolution with a recorded reason. [assumed] (assumption)
### W9 Sale confirmation and handover (release 1) — actors: R6 Sales Executive, R5 Finance Officer, R1 Administrator, R2 Project Manager; trigger: A booked unit reaches its recorded sale milestone.; outcome: Sale and handover have distinct, auditable completion records.; how often: As each booking reaches its milestones. [assumed, high impact] (assumption, Q9, Q10)
1. Sales records the signed agreement and milestone details on the active booking.
2. Finance verifies the booking's required payments against posted receipts.
3. An independent authorized Finance Officer or Administrator confirms the sale.
4. The application atomically changes the unit from booked to sold and records the evidence.
5. The Project Manager records completion of the handover checklist.
6. Finance clears outstanding handover payment requirements and Sales records the handover date.
- Missing evidence or unmet payment requirements block completion without changing unit availability.

## Rules
- BR1 Authorization is deny-by-default and server-determined with one role per user and project-scoped access; browser values never grant access.. Applies: A direct API request for an unassigned project's document is denied.. Does not apply: An authorized request within the user's assigned project can proceed. (description, doc:Greenfield-Angular-Go-SQLite-Requirements.md)
- BR2 Prevent removal or deactivation of the final active administrator.. Applies: Deactivating the only active administrator is rejected.. Does not apply: Deactivating an administrator is not barred by this rule when another active administrator remains. (doc:Greenfield-Angular-Go-SQLite-Requirements.md)
- BR3 Role changes, project assignment changes, deactivation, and password resets invalidate affected sessions or force permission re-evaluation before the next protected operation.. Applies: An existing session cannot continue reading a project after its assignment is removed.. Does not apply: An unchanged authorized session remains subject to normal session expiry. (doc:Greenfield-Angular-Go-SQLite-Requirements.md)
- BR4 Prevent negative stock, incompatible quantity units, and receipts above ordered quantities unless an authorized amendment changes the order; transfers are atomic across both sites.. Applies: An issue exceeding the site's available stock is rejected.. Does not apply: An issue within the available quantity in the correct unit can proceed with permission. (doc:Greenfield-Angular-Go-SQLite-Requirements.md)
- BR5 Prevent payment over-allocation and cross-project or cross-customer allocation; derive outstanding balances from posted invoices, valid allocations, and reversals.. Applies: A receipt cannot be allocated to a different customer's invoice.. Does not apply: Allocation to the same customer's invoice in the same project within the available amount can proceed. (doc:Greenfield-Angular-Go-SQLite-Requirements.md)
- BR6 Use integer currency minor units and one company base currency; define fractional quantity and price scale and rounding; reverse posted financial records with a reason instead of editing or deleting them.. Applies: A correction to a posted receipt requires a traceable reversal.. Does not apply: A draft invoice is not yet subject to the posted-record reversal requirement. (doc:Greenfield-Angular-Go-SQLite-Requirements.md)
- BR7 Enforce one active reservation/booking relationship per property unit using transaction and database safeguards; release units only through authorized expiry or cancellation.. Applies: A second simultaneous buyer cannot obtain an active booking for an already secured unit.. Does not apply: An available unit without an active relationship can be reserved by an authorized user. (doc:Greenfield-Angular-Go-SQLite-Requirements.md)
- BR8 Cancellation does not imply a refund; preserve receipts and record refunds separately.. Applies: Cancelling a paid booking leaves its original receipts intact.. Does not apply: A booking with no collected money does not need a refund of collections. (doc:Greenfield-Angular-Go-SQLite-Requirements.md)
- BR9 Link expense and bill source records to count costs once; distinguish physical progress, booked/sold units, approved costs, commitments, invoiced amounts, and cash collections; preserve historic amounts after asking-price changes.. Applies: A bill linked to an already counted expense must not add the same cost again.. Does not apply: An independent approved cost with no previously counted source contributes its own amount. (doc:Greenfield-Angular-Go-SQLite-Requirements.md)
- BR10 Calculate cancellation refunds using the cancellation terms recorded on the booking, without treating cancellation as payment of a refund.. Applies: A cancelled booking with collections uses its recorded terms to determine the refundable amount.. Does not apply: An active booking does not trigger a cancellation refund calculation. (Q5)
- BR11 Staff enter invoice tax amounts rather than having the application calculate tax from configured rates.. Applies: Staff enter the tax amount when preparing an invoice that includes tax.. Does not apply: The application does not automatically derive tax from a company tax rate. (Q6)
- BR12 Use transactional state checks and persistent duplicate protection for non-repeatable submissions; version-check important edits and return a safe 409 Conflict for stale edits or invalid concurrent transitions. Approvals and their audit events commit together.. Applies: A stale edit to an important record is rejected rather than silently overwriting a newer change.. Does not apply: A current authorized edit with a valid transition may commit. (doc:Greenfield-Angular-Go-SQLite-Requirements.md)
- BR13 A forced password-setup state permits only setup completion and logout.. Applies: A newly created account requiring setup cannot access project records before setting its password.. Does not apply: An account with completed setup can access work allowed by its role and assignments. (doc:Greenfield-Angular-Go-SQLite-Requirements.md)
- BR14 Only an authorized Finance Officer or Administrator may confirm the sale milestone, and the confirmer must not be the booking creator.. Applies: A Finance Officer independently confirms a booking created by a Sales Executive in an assigned project.. Does not apply: A booking creator cannot confirm their own booking even if their role otherwise permits confirmation. [assumed, high impact] (Q9, assumption)
- BR15 Confirming a sale requires an active booking, an attached signed sale agreement, a milestone date and recorded Finance verification of the booking's payment requirements against posted receipts; retain confirmer, timestamp and evidence references.. Applies: An active booking with its signed agreement and verified required deposit can become sold even when later installments remain due.. Does not apply: A cancelled booking or a booking missing its signed agreement cannot become sold. [assumed, high impact] (Q10, assumption)
- BR16 Record a reservation expiry explicitly using a 48-hour default. Conversion changes reserved to booked; expiry or approved cancellation returns the unit to available. Only an unreserved and unbooked unit can be manually marked unavailable. Sold units require an approved cancellation before release.. Applies: An expired unconverted reservation releases its unit automatically.. Does not apply: An already converted reservation cannot release a booked unit. [assumed, high impact] (assumption)
